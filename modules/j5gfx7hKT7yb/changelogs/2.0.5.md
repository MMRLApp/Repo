**Warmth reaches the warm end again.** Drag the warmth slider straight to its warmest, or take SonoLune's Light room to 100%, and on some phones nothing happened: the screen kept the warmth it already had, and nudging the slider back a step put it right. The cause is in Android. Its colour service remembers the last temperature its own Night Light slider gave it, and ignores any later setting that matches it, until the phone restarts. With this module's overlay in place, the warm end of Android's own slider is 1700K, which is also the warm end of every slider that drives this module. So once Android's own slider had been taken to its warmest, every request for 1700K was stored and then ignored. Measured on a Nothing Phone (2): after 3000K, a write of 1700K left the screen at 3000K. Lune now writes a value one kelvin away first, then the real one. Android can ignore at most one of the two, so the screen lands on what you asked for or one kelvin beside it, which nobody can see. If you drive Lune Bridge from SonoLune, this is the whole fix, and SonoLune does not need an update.

**The panel's warmth takes turns.** The warmth slider waited for your finger to rest, then started a change and read the settings back when it finished, and the presets started theirs straight away. Nothing stopped a second change from starting while the first was still running, so a drag that paused, or a preset tapped right after a drag, could finish in either order and leave the screen on the older value. The read-back could also pull the thumb back under a finger that was still moving. Now only one change runs at a time, the newest value waits behind it, and the last thing you touched is always the last thing applied. The screen follows the thumb while you drag, and a preset moves the thumb the moment you tap it.

**Commands that overlap no longer trip over each other.** Every lunectl command that changes the display now waits for the one before it, and the settings file is written so that two writers can never corrupt it. The wait can never be what keeps your screen dark: a command that died is stepped over at once, and one that is stuck gets about five seconds before Lune carries on without it. `lunectl warm` now also says so, and exits non-zero, when Android refuses a temperature, instead of recording a warmth the screen is not showing.
